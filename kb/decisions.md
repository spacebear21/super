- **Questions for the human carry their substance, never ids** (2026-11-05, on the triage decision sheet). A question must say in plain words what the code does, what goes wrong and who is hurt, or what a class checks and what the drafts under it claim, then the options and a recommended answer with its reason. Finding ids, class ids and file paths go in a trailing reference line only. Merge questions that are one decision in substance, order by how much the answer changes, and aim for tens of questions, not a hundred. The human: "I'm a human that can't index 200 findings and memorize ids. I need a human-worded question that gets at the root of the issue."

- **SatoshiPortal/payjoin is the highest priority repository** (human, 2026-11-06, for that night's run). Acted on at once: the 34 BIP77 classes the sheet proposed for integrations got the integration level on master and the areas allowlists of satoshiportal-payjoin and bull-bitcoin-mobile were cleared, ahead of the human's answers to the decision sheet, because without them the project matched two classes. The rest of the sheet waits for the answers.

- **The BTCPay async payjoin plugin is critical infrastructure** (human, 2026-11-06): it runs the payjoin.org/donate server. Audit it fully and treat its findings as top priority with Bull Bitcoin's.
- **Severity of a sender that drops the pj URL path is low, unless the wallet advertises itself as a payjoin wallet, then medium or high** (human, 2026-11-06). A split payment and payjoin server deployment that must signal pjos=0 is medium to high depending on the setup and the trust it requires. An HTTP 500 on an original input without UTXO data is low.
- **Projects not worth auditing** (human, 2026-11-06): PrivateBit, btc-go-bip353, Cypher Box and wallet-hunter go to watch. camlcoin is watch and none of its findings gets reported. The six non-Payjoin projects (Samourai extlibj, SideSwap Rust, Fabric, NoBank, Shrike, privkeyio-drongo) are excluded.
- **The reading list is decided after the audit has covered the important projects**, not before: SatoshiPortal/payjoin, Bull mobile and the BTCPay async plugin first.

- **No silent fallback to expensive models, and guard rails on the failure rate** (human, 2026-11-06, after the keyless cron run spent 47 minutes on Claude and Codex): "we should definitely have guard rails if like 90% of calls are failing. at the very least a notification. we don't want to be falling back silently to more expensive models." So: a run that is mostly failing notifies and stops; every call a minority model answers because the primaries could not is counted, logged and shown in the summary, and a notification goes out when it happens at all.

No pre-filter for candidates: the strong triage of noisy candidates is fine as long as the plan and the audit start at once from the registry as it stands, and triaged projects enter the registry for the next run. The human: "i dont even think we need the pre filter triage if we don't block the rest of the scan."

- **Review protocol for decision sheets** (human, 2026-11-06): the human comments inline in the sheet file with "> " quote lines under the rows; any question without a comment is approved as recommended. - **Interactive agent work tolerates no model slower than about five seconds a call** (human, 2026-11-06, after kimi-k3.1-pro's two-minute queue stalled the interop research for two hours). Measure before choosing: on 2026-11-06 20:15 UTC a 25k-token prompt with 200 output tokens took deepseek-v4.1-flash 1.8 s, minimax-m3.1-flash 1.4 s, nemotron-3-ultra 2.5 s, nemotron-3-super 2.9 s, glm-5.3-flash 3.6 s, nemotron-3.5-lightning 5.1 s, deepseek uncensored 5.6 s, glm-5.3 and its uncensored twin 6 s, kimi-k3-abliterated 6.5 s, glm-5.3-flash uncensored 6.9 s, and kimi-k3.1-pro 95 s. Research and worker agents on Token Factory run on glm-5.3 (strong) or deepseek-v4.1-flash (fast) until kimi recovers; the audit's strong order leads with glm-5.3 for the same reason.
- **Audit pull requests before they merge** (human, 2026-11-06). Watch projects whose Payjoin support is prerelease or a proof of concept are to be audited through their open pull requests, pre-merge, because a critical issue is cheaper to flag before a release than after. On active projects the audit is to look for open pull requests that touch Payjoin logic and check that they introduce no new issue. Applies to the payjoin-audit pipeline; PR findings are reported on the PR, not as issues against released code.
