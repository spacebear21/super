Briefs must say so when a task involves reading many files.

- **Questions for the human carry their substance, never ids** (2026-11-05, on the triage decision sheet). A question must say in plain words what the code does, what goes wrong and who is hurt, or what a class checks and what the drafts under it claim, then the options and a recommended answer with its reason. Finding ids, class ids and file paths go in a trailing reference line only. Merge questions that are one decision in substance, order by how much the answer changes, and aim for tens of questions, not a hundred. The human: "I'm a human that can't index 200 findings and memorize ids. I need a human-worded question that gets at the root of the issue."

- **SatoshiPortal/payjoin is the highest priority repository** (human, 2026-11-06, for that night's run). Acted on at once: the 34 BIP77 classes the sheet proposed for integrations got the integration level on master and the areas allowlists of satoshiportal-payjoin and bull-bitcoin-mobile were cleared, ahead of the human's answers to the decision sheet, because without them the project matched two classes. The rest of the sheet waits for the answers.

- **The BTCPay async payjoin plugin is critical infrastructure** (human, 2026-11-06): it runs the payjoin.org/donate server. Audit it fully and treat its findings as top priority with Bull Bitcoin's.
- **Severity of a sender that drops the pj URL path is low, unless the wallet advertises itself as a payjoin wallet, then medium or high** (human, 2026-11-06). A split payment and payjoin server deployment that must signal pjos=0 is medium to high depending on the setup and the trust it requires. An HTTP 500 on an original input without UTXO data is low.
- **Projects not worth auditing** (human, 2026-11-06): PrivateBit, btc-go-bip353, Cypher Box and wallet-hunter go to watch. camlcoin is watch and none of its findings gets reported. The six non-Payjoin projects (Samourai extlibj, SideSwap Rust, Fabric, NoBank, Shrike, privkeyio-drongo) are excluded.
- **The reading list is decided after the audit has covered the important projects**, not before: SatoshiPortal/payjoin, Bull mobile and the BTCPay async plugin first.
